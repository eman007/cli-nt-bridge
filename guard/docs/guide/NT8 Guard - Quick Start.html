Quick start

NT8 Guard

Keeps NinjaTrader 8 running: starts it and logs in after a crash, restarts it when it freezes, recovers a dead feed, puts back dropped strategies - and tells your phone.

Step 1

Install

Extract the zip. Double-click Install.cmd. The setup wizard opens.

Step 2

Check & log in

Fix anything red. Tick Remember username in NinjaTrader once, then save your password.

Step 3

Add-ons

Be flat. Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On → NT8Guard-AddOns.zip. Wait for two green lights.

Step 4

Alerts

Scan the QR code with the ntfy app. Keep the Recover NT8 preset, or pick another.

Step 5

Start

Finish setup. Then the fire drill, while the market is open and you are flat.

Where things are

Start menu → NT8 Guard
%LOCALAPPDATA%\NT8Guard settings, password, logs
Weekend drill → NinjaTrader's Simulated Data Feed

Good to know

  • Closing NinjaTrader on purpose? Pause first, or Guard starts it again within a minute.
  • It never disconnects an account holding a position.
  • Switching strategies back on is your call by default: Guard alerts.
  • Keep the PC awake; a locked screen holds the login.
